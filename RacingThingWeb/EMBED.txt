Putting Drifters on another website
===================================

Replace YOURGAME.onrender.com with your Render address. Either way the game still
connects back to your Render server for multiplayer, as if it was on the real site.

1) As a frame (simplest). Paste this into the other site's page:

<iframe src="https://YOURGAME.onrender.com/"
        style="border:0; width:100%; height:100vh"
        allow="autoplay; fullscreen; gamepad"></iframe>

2) Loaded in as the page itself (no frame). Make a page on the other site with just this:

<!doctype html>
<html><body><script>
fetch('https://YOURGAME.onrender.com/')
  .then(r => r.text())
  .then(html => { document.open(); document.write(html); document.close(); });
</script></body></html>

Notes
- Your server needs the new server.js / WebRelay.cs from this update (it lets other
  sites fetch the page and tells the game where home is).
- Free Render sites go to sleep: the first visit after a while can take ~30 s.
- Best times are saved per website, so they won't carry over between the two.
