How to play

Everyone is a ball. Knock the others off the map or into anything red. Last one alive wins the round.

Keys

← → ↑ ↓ (or WASD) roll and float

X (or Shift) go heavy: you hit much harder and get pushed less, but you're slow to steer

Z (or Space) special move, depends on the mode

Enter chat   Esc menu

Modes

Classic push and go heavy.

Simple same, but nobody can go heavy.

Arrows hold Z to draw your bow, aim with ←→, let go to shoot. The longer you hold, the harder it flies. Arrows knock people back.

Death Arrows same as Arrows, but one hit and you're out. Watch your own arrows too.

Grapple hold Z to shoot a rope at the nearest thing in reach and get yanked towards it. Grab other players to pull them around.

VTOL you're a little jet. ←→ turn, ↑ thrust.

Football red vs blue. Bop the ball into the other team's goal.
